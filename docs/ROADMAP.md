## Next
1. **Balance with real play.** Follow [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md) to collect human playtests and set a target win rate per difficulty. The scripts can already craft ammo and build an economy, and the measurements say ammo is **not** the bottleneck: difficulty has to come from the early economy, structural defence or nights 3–4, not from the last horde (see [BALANCE.md](BALANCE.md)). Any claim smaller than ten points needs 60–100 seeds per arm.
2. **Region overlays** (logistics, power).
3. **Real maps, next steps.** A map view (MapLibre) to pick the area by dragging, water and rail, multipolygon buildings with courtyards, a road graph for long trips on large areas, and an offline importer for bigger regions.
4. **Logistics and policies:** hauling between storages, "keep N food" policies, priority UI for tasks.
5. **Research tree** (survival, construction, energy, defense, society).
6. **Energy and water networks**, vehicles.
7. **Performance:** chunk streaming and LOD for large imported cities, simulation in a Web Worker, aggregated distant hordes.
8. **Settlements and diplomacy:** the "rebuild society" stage.

## Done — art and asset pipeline (ArtCraft apps and Blender)
- [x] **Surface textures.** Four tileable maps (concrete, asphalt, grass, rust) generated by `tools/make-textures.ts`, applied with world-space UVs on buildings and the merged ground; a missing file falls back to the flat colour.
- [x] **Flipbook impact effects.** The `hit_sparks_final` render packed into an atlas and played by the burst pool, replacing the procedural gradient (0.081% of the screen before).
- [x] **Low-poly prop kit in Blender.** Car, dumpster, fence, sign, lamp post and barrier (12–148 triangles, 30 KB), instanced per part; 324 props placed deterministically on the default map.
- [x] **Survivors with a real silhouette.** `walker.glb` (244 triangles) instanced in place of the capsule, three draw calls for the whole squad.
- [x] **Night look.** Lamp-post halos in one `InstancedMesh` and fog that closes in after dark. No bloom pass.
- [x] **HUD and minimap.** Inline SVG resource icons, horde entry markers on the minimap after dusk, and a rendered survivor portrait in the side panel.
- [x] **A pipeline with a contract.** `manifest.json` with size and `sha256`; `pnpm assets:check` re-derives byte budget, PNG header, alpha channel and GLB header from disk and runs in CI.
- [x] **A flipbook per event.** Shots play a muzzle flash, deaths a cloud of dust and dried blood, work ticks pale dust, infected strikes three claw streaks, gunfire a ring spreading over the ground to the radius that pulls infected (45 m pistol, 70 m rifle), and a fallen wall, tower or gate a dome of rubble dust with masonry thrown up, all rendered in EffectCraft from recipes in `tools/effectcraft/`; the spark is left to hits. Clouds blend normally and replace the particles of their event, the strike replaces its particles too and mirrors every other slash, noise rings lie flat and merge when shots come from within 10 m (three alive at most), collapses are capped at four, and construction dust is capped at six alive so a busy site never takes the slots shots and deaths need (decision 21).
- [x] **`tools/make-flipbook-atlas.ts`.** Packs a raw RGBA sequence into an atlas, rejects an empty frame and records grid and per-frame coverage in the manifest. Soft effects ship at 96 px cells or every other frame when 128 px would cost more than 100 KB.
- [x] **Textured ground keeps its colour.** The ground materials kept their flat colour under the map and were darkened twice (asphalt at sRGB 10 instead of 52); each now takes a tint derived from its map's mean, pinned by a test.
- [x] **Frame cost probe.** `GameController.cost` and `tests/e2e/perf.spec.ts` split simulation from render; results and their limits in [PERF.md](PERF.md).

Two constraints learned the hard way still apply to every new asset:

- **No video with alpha reaches either engine.** Godot 4.7.2 has no `VideoStreamWebm`, and an OGV transcode loses alpha (`Requested planes not available`). Anything animated goes PNG sequence → atlas → flipbook, never a video file.
- **No asset enters the repository unverified.** Every asset is listed in `manifest.json` and checked by `pnpm assets:check`; a render once claimed by an agent was fabricated and left no file on disk.

## Next — art

1. **Measure on a real GPU.** Every frame number so far comes from SwiftShader. The 60 fps budget below has not been checked on hardware, nor with a night horde on screen.
2. **Bloom on emissive materials** (windows, halos, infected eyes), only if the GPU measurement leaves room for it.
3. **A facade texture** and a single atlas for the surface maps; today each map is its own file.
4. **Billboard atlas at distance versus GLB up close** for survivors and infected, decided by draw calls and frame time with forty units on screen.
5. **Infected with a real model.** They are still capsules; `walker.glb` was authored as an infected, so the model already exists.
6. **See the effects in a real fight.** Muzzle flash, strike, noise ring, death cloud, collapse and dust were reviewed only with events fired by script in daylight; a night horde against a walled base is the review that counts. The work-tick dust also shows on farms and workshops, which suits building and repair better.

## Budget

Every art item has to be measured against a declared ceiling, otherwise "looks better" wins every argument:

- **Initial payload ≤ 1.5 MB.** The JS bundle is 976 kB raw and 276 kB gzip (2026-11-05, up from 869/245 kB before the art pass); the assets in `public/art` are 864 KB after the six flipbooks, against the 1 MB ceiling `pnpm assets:check` enforces; atlases are shared and tiled, and nothing ships per object.
- **60 fps at 1080p** with about 100 buildings, forty survivors and one night horde. Shadows, bloom and particle counts are the three knobs that move this number.
- **Audio and large textures stay out of the bundle:** sound streams from `public/sfx` (372 KB), and no texture over 64 kB is inlined.
- An asset that cannot be measured against these numbers does not ship.
